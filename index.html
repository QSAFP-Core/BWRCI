The Self-Regulatory Organization

One house. One standard.
Every initiative answers to it.

BWRCI is founded on the principle that when intelligence and wealth are securely and widely distributed, humanity gains unprecedented capacity to comprehend and improve our world. As the coalition's SRO, BWRCI holds each initiative below to a shared baseline — before any regulator has to ask.

I

Common Standards

One baseline for every charter: machines act only with authority humans grant, and economic value flows back to the people who create it.

II

Claims Discipline

Every public claim is labeled as demonstrated, simulated or proposed. Evidence leads; bold statements follow.

III

Curated Participation

Public entries, drawings and sign-ups across the coalition route through BWRCI as curator, under one data policy.

IV

Independence & Attribution

Naming an AI developer or partner never implies endorsement. Collaborators are credited, and the human keeps the final say.

OCUPOne Chip Unified Protocol

The Authority Layer

“Machines may exercise delegated authority, but must not become the source of their own continued authority.”

Temporal authority governance turns policy promises into testable boundaries. Authority is bounded in time and capability, checked at runtime, and expires unless humans renew it.

Quorum ValidatorNetwork

The Human Quorum

QVN is empowering humanity over AI. Authority granted through OCUP runs on a lease; when the lease runs out, a pool of human validators decides whether the AI node continues. Every AI node is a contract — every reauthorization, a renewal of human trust.

LeaseBounded permission to operate.
ExpireNo self-extension — ever.
QuorumHumans reauth or deny, in public.

The QVN Validator Network Demo
⚡ Interactive ⚡

You will be able to interact wherever you see the check. ✅
  1. Biometric general logon
  2. Renewal review assigned to random validator pool, first 3 responders win
  3. Biometric confirmation of winners to continue to secured logs
  4. Responders individually review logs ✅ and vote to Reauth or Deny ✅
  5. The system records the vote, rewards the validator, states payment within 24 hrs.
  6. RESOLUTION IS PUBLIC — AI NODE REAUTHED or AI NODE DENIED
